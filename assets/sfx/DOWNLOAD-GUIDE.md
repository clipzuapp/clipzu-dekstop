# Free SFX Resources for Content Creators

## Quick Download Links

Since automated downloading has limitations, here are the best FREE and LEGAL sources where you can quickly download SFX:

### 1. **Mixkit** (Recommended - No Attribution Required)
- URL: https://mixkit.co/free-sound-effects/
- License: Free for commercial use
- Categories: Transitions, UI, Effects, Ambient
- **Download**: Click any sound → Download button

**Essential Packs:**
- Whooshes: https://mixkit.co/free-sound-effects/whoosh/
- UI Sounds: https://mixkit.co/free-sound-effects/interface/
- Impacts: https://mixkit.co/free-sound-effects/impact/

### 2. **Pixabay Audio** (CC0 - Public Domain)
- URL: https://pixabay.com/sound-effects/
- License: Free for commercial use, no attribution required
- **Direct Search Links:**
  - Pop sounds: https://pixabay.com/sound-effects/search/pop/
  - Whoosh: https://pixabay.com/sound-effects/search/whoosh/
  - Click: https://pixabay.com/sound-effects/search/click/
  - Ding: https://pixabay.com/sound-effects/search/ding/
  - Notification: https://pixabay.com/sound-effects/search/notification/

### 3. **Freesound.org** (CC0 Filter)
- URL: https://freesound.org/search/?q=whoosh&f=license:"Creative+Commons+0"
- Filter by: Creative Commons 0
- Requires free account

### 4. **YouTube Audio Library** (Free)
- URL: https://studio.youtube.com/ (if you have YouTube account)
- Navigate to: Audio Library → Sound Effects
- Filter: Attribution not required
